# WEC Simulation Visualizer User Guide
## Core Concepts

**Missions and Environment**
There are four distinct missions, each with unique constraints, run limits, and environmental challenges (like varying wind speeds and turbulence). Select your active mission in the **Mission** tab within the Control Console to view its specific constraints.

**Physical Specs**
Configure your wind turbine's structural geometry here. You can adjust the **Hub Height**, **Top/Base Diameter**, and **Wall Thickness**. These parameters directly impact the turbine's structural safety, material mass, and capital expenditure (CAPEX).

**Drivetrain**
Customize the internal mechanics by selecting a **Gearbox** and **Generator** type. Your choices here will balance power generation efficiency against component weight and long-term maintenance costs (OPEX).

**Results**
Simulation outputs are displayed in the **Analytics & Results** panel, divided into Performance, Structural, and Financial Ledger tabs. Your goal is to optimize the turbine to pass all mission constraints within the limited number of R&D runs allowed.

---

## Using the Program

1. **Authentication:** To begin testing, enter your Name and a valid SSN in the **Mission** tab. This securely personalizes your simulation parameters.
2. **UI Preferences:** Adjust the interface scale (Zoom) and toggle the appearance theme using the controls in the top header bar. 
3. **Configuration:** Tweak your turbine variables under the **Physical Specs** and **Drivetrain** tabs to design your ideal setup.
4. **Simulation:** Once your parameters are ready, click **Run Simulation** (located at the bottom of the Physical Specs tab).
5. **Review:** Check the **Analytics & Results** panel to analyze your performance metrics, structural safety alerts, and financial breakdown. Return to the **Mission** tab to see which constraints you successfully passed! 

**Challenge to the users:**
As a challenge, try to pass as many of these as possible before your R&D runs have run out for all the missions!
